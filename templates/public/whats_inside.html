Why Pure Kansa (Bronze) Cookware?

At Hotel Rhino, your food is served exclusively in traditional hand-beaten bronze and brass plates (Kansa Thali) crafted by heritage metalsmiths. Vedic tradition considers Kansa an alloy of copper and tin that naturally alkalizes food, stimulates gut enzymes, and keeps dishes warm throughout your meal.
