Himalayan Authenticity

Why Hotel Rhino's Thakali Khana is Loved by Connoisseurs

The Thakali community hails from the Thak Khola region of Mustang district along the historic Himalayan salt trade route. Their cooking is known throughout Nepal as the pinnacle of balanced, flavorful, and deeply satisfying food.

Hand-Harvested Himalayan Jimbu

A mountain allium herb dried in the cold air of Mustang, fried in piping hot pure cow ghee to release its unique nutty, savory aroma.

Pure Brass (Kansa) Cookware & Serving

According to Vedic science and Thakali custom, dining in heavy bell-metal brass promotes digestion, enhances food temperature retention, and preserves micronutrients.

True Hospitality & Unlimited Refills

No Thakali meal is complete without warm refills of hot steamed rice, Mustang dal, fresh saag, and savory curry ladled right at your table with a smile.

Mustang Ingredients

Sourced directly from local farmers in Thak Khola and delivered to our Dhangadhi kitchen.
