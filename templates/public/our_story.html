Generational Gastronomy

The Flavours of
Our Land

In the fertile plains and mist-draped hills of Sudurpaschim, food is not merely sustenance — it is a sacred offering of hospitality. Deeply rooted in indigenous culinary practices, every pot stirred in our kitchen honours centuries-old agrarian balance.

From the wild sun-dried herbs of the far-western valleys to the black lentils tempered with native Mustang Jimbu and local Shingati butter, our recipes follow the celestial rhythms of Nepal's seasonal harvest. We never use commercial powders, additives, or synthetic enhancers — our only secrets are stone-milled Silauto pastes, mountain river minerals, and slow wooden fire.

Ancestral Culinary Stages

From Our Roots to Your Plate

The authentic four-step ritual practiced daily in the kitchens of Hotel Rhino.

01

Fresh Local Sourcing

Grown in Sudurpaschim's pristine valleys and Upper Mustang's micro-climates without synthetic agro-chemicals.

02

Stone Silauto Grinding

Crushed gently on stone mortars to release essential fragrant oils that modern electric blenders destroy.

03

Slow Wood-Fire Cooking

Simmered in heavy bell-metal brass deghs, infusing authentic smoky warmth and tenderizing meats gently.

04

Sacred Kansa Serving

Served gracefully on heavy bronze thalis, finished with sizzling hot cow ghee poured before your eyes.
