The Story of Hotel Rhino

From the dry winds of Thak Khola to the warm hearts of our dining room.

The Himalayan Salt Route Tradition

Centuries ago, along the rugged Kali Gandaki gorge dividing the majestic Annapurna and Dhaulagiri mountain massifs, the indigenous Thakali people of Mustang managed the bustling trade between Tibet and the southern plains of Nepal.

To nourish weary caravans, traders, and pilgrims, Thakali mothers and innkeepers developed an exceptional culinary mastery: a complete, impeccably balanced thali designed to restore vitality after long mountain journeys. They elevated simple ingredients — mountain buckwheat, black lentils, wild Jimbu herb, and locally pressed mustard oil — into Nepal’s most beloved gastronomic triumph.

Our Commitment at Hotel Rhino

Hotel Rhino was born with a singular, uncompromising purpose: to preserve and honor authentic Thakali cuisine without dilution or shortcuts.

  • No artificial colors, MSG, or processed preservatives: Only pure spices ground on traditional stone Silauto.
  • True Mustang Jimbu: Hand-picked in the sub-alpine regions of Upper Mustang.
  • Authentic Brassware: Solid hand-cast bell-metal Kansa sets that maintain heat and celebrate dining as an auspicious ritual.
  • Unconditional Hospitality: The Nepali spirit of "Atithi Devo Bhava" (The guest is god) with endless hot refills of rice, dal, and greens.
